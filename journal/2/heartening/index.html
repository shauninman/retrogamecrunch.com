Heartening

Posted by Shaun 2013/01/04 12:24:01

Less than 12 hours remain to pick your favorite themes for our first crunch! We received over 500 usable themes. Because of this surprising volume, we curated a shortlist of 21 themes we found most interesting or just had too many duplicate submissions to ignore. (You can still see and pick from the rest by clicking the See All button.)

Next time, we’ll probably limit theme submissions to 3 per person and increase the duration of both the submission and voting phases.

Lastly, we finally published the final pre-crunch Primer, Sound Off! It’s short but sweet and features a new track (also short but sweet) from Matt called Genki (私は元気です、ありがとう).
