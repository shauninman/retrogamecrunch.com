A Boy and His Pug

Posted by Shaun 2013/07/16 22:04:55

Time for more art! Oontz (who is starting to look like a white collar DJ) is joined by his pet pug, Wub-Wub. (Inspired by this Vine from Adam Saltsman) Wub-Wub will probably be a power-up who, once collected, you’ll control at the same time as Oontz. He can do anything Oontz can except toss record sleeves.

Also adding some more bad drops. The Sun will melt any on-screen records if allowed to set. The Twister will blow any on-screen records left or right if allowed to touch down.

Both have full screen animated effects that I’m going to hold off on revealing until the complete version is done.

Edit Forgot to mention, I’m trying a new art style with this game: no outlines. It seems silly to waste pixels and colors on outlines when there are so few to work with.
